# Legal Risk Analysis & Gap Assessment

**Analysis Date:** October 29, 2025

This document identifies 30 potential legal risks and gaps in the current documentation, categorized by severity.

## CRITICAL RISKS (3) - Immediate Attention Required

### 1. Banking License and Regulatory Status
**Gap:** Terms don't clearly state if NeoBank is a licensed bank or fintech partner.
**Risk:** Users may assume FDIC insurance that doesn't exist.
**Solution:** Create Regulatory Disclosure stating exact legal structure and banking partnerships.

### 2. Securities Trading Registration  
**Gap:** No clarification of broker-dealer status, FINRA membership, or SIPC coverage.
**Risk:** Operating as unregistered broker-dealer is a federal crime.
**Solution:** Add Securities Disclosure with broker relationships and SIPC insurance details.

### 3. Cryptocurrency Regulatory Compliance
**Gap:** Lacks state money transmitter licenses, FinCEN registration, custody details.
**Risk:** State enforcement actions and customer losses without insurance.
**Solution:** Create Cryptocurrency Addendum with state availability and regulatory disclosures.
